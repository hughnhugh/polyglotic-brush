# 0009 Stroke-order data ships as separate files under its own licences

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-21
- **Decided by:** the user with Claude, 2026-11-20 (polyglotic-hq project 520's plan, section 2; DECISIONS #232)

## Situation

Make Me a Hanzi and animCJK are under the Arphic Public License (copyleft: changed files are published under the same terms); KanjiVG is CC BY-SA 3.0 (a compiled form is likely an adaptation, and it forbids technical measures that restrict recipients).

## Decision

No stroke data is in this repository or compiled into the engine. When it comes (M2 on), it is separate files with their licences and credits beside them, our corrections published under the same terms, never encrypted.

## Why

The engine and the app keep their own licences.

## What would reverse it, and how

A licence reading that allows more; none is expected.
